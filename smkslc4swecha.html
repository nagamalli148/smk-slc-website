SMKSLC4SWECHA

A Campus Open-Source Community

A student-led space connecting SDMSMK SLC with the Swecha community through learning, collaboration and contribution.

THE COMMUNITY

Built by Students, Open to Everyone

SMKSLC4SWECHA brings students together to explore free and open-source software, exchange knowledge and work on meaningful projects.

It is a place for beginners and experienced learners alike to ask questions, find collaborators and take their first steps into community-driven technology.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Find Your Way In

Learn

Join workshops, learning hours and technical sessions.

Build

Work with a team on projects and hackathon ideas.

Contribute

Share skills, improve solutions and support other learners.
