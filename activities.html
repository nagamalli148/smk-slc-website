ONE YEAR OF ACTIVITIES

Learning & Innovation

Explore the workshops, orientations, hackathons and technical activities conducted through the SLC.

01

05 August 2025

Orientation about Swecha

Participation: 150–200 students from II B.Sc Computer Science (Sections A & B), II B.Sc Artificial Intelligence, and BCA.

Description: An orientation session introducing Swecha AP, its free and open-source philosophy, and opportunities for students to learn by contributing to real-time projects.

Outcome: 150+ students were introduced to Swecha and the importance of free software in building solutions for societal problems. A SMK SLC community and signup link were also created for interested students.

02

20–23 August 2025

Design Thinking & Innovation

Participation: 109 students from II B.Sc Computer Science (Sections A & B) and II B.Sc Artificial Intelligence.

Description: A four-day workshop where students worked in teams to brainstorm ideas and develop prototype designs using design-thinking practices. The fourth day included an Ideathon.

Themes: Health & Wellbeing, Women & Empowerment, Agriculture, Rural Development, Education, and Environment & Sustainability.

Outcome: 109+ participants gained knowledge of design-thinking phases and were introduced to Agile, Git and Excalidraw for wireframing.

03

17 September 2025

SIH Work Session

Participation: 50 students from II B.Sc Computer Science (Sections A & B) and II B.Sc Artificial Intelligence.

Description: A preparatory work session for the Smart India Hackathon in which students formed teams and selected real-time problem statements to ideate solutions.

Outcome: 7+ teams participated and selected real-time problem statements to develop presentations according to the relevant guidelines.

04

20 September 2025

Internal Hackathon (SIH)

Participation: 60 students organised as 10 teams of 6.

Description: An internal hackathon conducted before a jury, where participating teams presented their ideas for selected real-time problem statements.

Outcome: 6–7 teams conducted thorough research and developed their ideas into presentations.
