ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Learning Through Participation

SDMSMK SLC provides a learning space where students can participate in workshops, hackathons, mentorship activities, internships and real-time projects.

Through its association with Swecha, students are introduced to free and open-source principles and encouraged to learn by doing and contributing to real-world problem solving.
