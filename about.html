ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

SWECHA & SDMSMK SLC

Creating opportunities for students to learn, contribute and innovate through open-source communities.

SWECHA

Learning Through Open Source

Swecha provides a community-oriented environment around free and open-source software. Students are encouraged to learn by participating in projects, workshops and collaborative technical activities.

The association with SDMSMK SLC creates opportunities for students to explore technology while developing practical problem-solving and collaboration skills.

SDMSMK SLC

A Space for Student Learning

The Siddhartha Mahila Kalasala SLC functions as a learning space where students can participate in workshops, hackathons, mentorship programmes, internships and real-time projects.

The focus is on learning through participation, experimentation and collaboration.

OUR VISION

Vision

To create a learning space on campus where every student becomes confident in contributing to a free and open-source community.

OUR MISSION

Mission

We conduct workshops, hackathons, mentorship programs, internships and real-time projects to help students learn by doing as a part of Siddhartha Mahila Kalasala SLC.

WHY OPEN SOURCE?

Learn. Collaborate. Contribute.

Collaboration

Students learn to work together and share knowledge through community participation.

Practical Learning

Workshops and projects allow students to apply concepts to practical problems.

Innovation

Students are encouraged to explore ideas, experiment and develop solutions.
